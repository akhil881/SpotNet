About SpotNet Services

Discover our mission to empower businesses and individuals with innovative solutions.

SpotNet Services is a premier provider of intelligent automation, advanced security solutions, and a wide range of products available through easy EMI options. Based in Churu, Rajasthan, we partner with Zebrs India OPC Private Limited to facilitate seamless purchases of electronics, smartphones, furniture, tyres, luggage, mattresses, electric vehicles, and more through trusted banks and NBFCs.

Our technology division specializes in AI-powered software, including lab reporting systems, GST filing automation, website builders, and custom APIs, designed to streamline operations and boost efficiency. Our security solutions include state-of-the-art CCTV systems, alarms, biometric access controls, and solar-powered setups, ensuring safety for homes, offices, and industries.

We work closely with vendors, requiring KYC documents, product serial numbers, proof of delivery, and Google Maps locations to ensure transparency and compliance. Our commission structure (2% + GST, 4.5% + GST for flour mills) supports rapid payment settlements within 1-2 days post-loan disbursement. At SpotNet, we are committed to driving innovation, security, and accessibility for all.
