How It Works

From Site Visit to Switch-On

A simple, transparent process — we manage everything so you don't have to.

1

Free Site Survey

Our team visits your rooftop, checks sunlight exposure, and studies your electricity bill to size the right system.

2

Custom Proposal

You get a clear quote with system size, expected savings, subsidy amount, and payback period — no hidden costs.

3

Installation

Certified technicians install panels, inverter, and wiring — typically completed within 3-7 days depending on system size.

4

Net-Metering & Go-Live

We coordinate with the local DISCOM for net-metering approval, then switch on your system and hand over documentation.
